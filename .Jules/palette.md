## 2026-06-12 - Accessibility and Interaction Polish
**Learning:** Adding ARIA roles and labels to modals and decorative icons significantly improves the accessibility posture of a landing page without changing the visual design. Tactile feedback via `active:scale-95` enhances the perceived responsiveness of the UI.
**Action:** Always implement body scroll locking and Escape key handling for custom React modals to ensure a professional UX. Use `aria-hidden="true"` for decorative SVGs to reduce screen reader noise.
