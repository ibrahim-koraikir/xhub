## 2024-06-26 - [Modal Accessibility and UX Enhancement]
**Learning:** For a complete accessible modal experience, besides ARIA roles and Escape key listeners, explicit focus-visible indicators for icon-only buttons (like the close button) are crucial to ensure keyboard users can clearly see which element is targeted, especially on dark-themed interfaces.
**Action:** Always include focus-visible styles with high contrast (like brand color rings) and appropriate offsets on modal interactive elements.
