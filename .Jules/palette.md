## 2025-05-14 - Implementing the 'Four-Pillar' Modal Pattern
**Learning:** Accessible modals require a holistic approach combining programmatic accessibility (ARIA), keyboard interaction (Escape key), environmental control (scroll locking), and semantic content structure (ordered lists for steps) to ensure a smooth experience for all users.
**Action:** Use the 'Four-Pillar' pattern for all future modal implementations to ensure consistent UX and accessibility standards.
