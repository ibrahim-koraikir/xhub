## 2025-07-08 - Modal Accessibility 'Four-Pillar' Pattern
**Learning:** A truly accessible modal requires four synchronized pillars: (1) ARIA attributes (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-labelledby`), (2) Keyboard support (Escape key closure), (3) Background scroll locking (`body` overflow hidden), and (4) Semantic content structure (e.g., using `<ol>` for sequential instructions).
**Action:** Apply these four pillars together in all future modal implementations to ensure a consistent and compliant UX.
